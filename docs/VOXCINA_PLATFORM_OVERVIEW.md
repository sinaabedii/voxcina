# Voxcina — Business Overview

> Plain-language guide to what Voxcina does, who it serves, how it makes money, and how it grows.
> Website: **voxcina.com**

---

## 1. At a Glance

**Voxcina is an online fashion store for Iran.**

We sell women's, men's and children's clothing — t-shirts, shirts, pants, dresses and more — directly to customers online. Our difference: customers can see how clothes look on their own photo before buying, get personal style advice in Persian, and even negotiate a personal discount in the cart.

**In one sentence:** Voxcina makes buying clothes online as confident and personal as buying in a real store.

---

## 2. The Problem We Solve

Buying clothes online is hard:

*   "Will this color suit me?"
*   "Will this size fit?"
*   "There are too many choices — what should I pick?"
*   "Can I trust the shop to deliver and accept returns?"

Voxcina removes these worries. Customers try clothes virtually, search by describing what they want in normal Persian, and get help from a styling assistant — all before they pay.

---

## 3. Business Model

### 3.1 What We Sell

We are a **single-brand / curated retail store** (not a marketplace with many sellers). Every product is sold by color and size.

For example, one "Oversized T-Shirt" appears as three separate choices — Black, White, Blue — each with its own photos and stock for sizes S, M, L, etc. This makes browsing simple: customers choose exactly what they see.

We also offer **seasonal collections** (Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter — بهار / تابستان / پاییز / زمستان) and curated bundles that group matching items together.

### 3.2 How We Make Money

1.  **Product Sales** — The main revenue. We buy / produce clothing and sell it at a margin. Price includes shipping; discounts are managed carefully.
2.  **Higher Basket Value** — Collections, "Complete the Look" recommendations, and trending carousels encourage customers to buy 2-3 items instead of one.
3.  **Affiliate Partners** — We give partners (influencers, sellers) a personal discount code. When their customers use it, the customer gets a discount and the partner earns a commission from a fixed shared pool. We only pay commission when the order is paid and kept (not cancelled or returned).
4.  **Repeat Purchases** — A 7-day easy return policy and good service turn first-time buyers into regular customers. Loyal customers cost less to keep than finding new ones.

### 3.3 Customer Journey

**Discover → Try → Decide → Buy → Receive → Come Back**

1.  Discover products on homepage, category, brand or collection pages, or via search
2.  Try items on their own photo or ask the AI stylist for advice
3.  Add to cart and negotiate a personal discount if they want
4.  Checkout with address (picked on a map), choose shipping, pay online
5.  Receive SMS updates, track order, get delivery
6.  Return within 7 days if needed, leave a review, save favorites for next time

## 5. What Customers Can Do — Key Features

### 5.1 Shopping Experience

*   **Easy browsing** — Clean catalog, filter by category, brand, price, popularity, new arrivals, or "in stock only". Each color is shown as its own card, so what you see is what you get.
*   **Rich product pages** — Multiple photos per color, sizes with live stock, material and fit details, and customer reviews (1-5 stars).
*   **Wishlist** — Save favorites and get notified when price drops or item is back in stock.
*   **Trending & New** — Homepage highlights most-viewed and newest items.

### 5.2 Confidence Before Buying (Our Main Advantage)

*   **Virtual Fitting Room** — Upload a photo and see any garment on yourself instantly. Saves time and reduces returns.
*   **AI Styling Assistant** — Chat in Persian: "Will this suit me? What pants match this shirt?" The assistant gives honest styling advice and suggests matching items from the catalog.
*   **Smart Search** — Type naturally, e.g. "a cool summer t-shirt", and get relevant results — no need to use exact filters.

### 5.3 Buying & After-Sales

*   **Smart Cart** — Works even before logging in; when you log in, your cart comes with you. If an item sells out, the cart tells you.
*   **Simple Checkout** — Pick address on the map, see real shipping costs by weight, apply a discount code.
*   **Trusted Payments** — Pay securely via Zibal, DigiPay, or SnappPay (including installment options). You can always check payment status.
*   **Order Tracking** — See order status in Persian (Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered), with dates in the Persian calendar.
*   **Easy Returns** — Request a return within 7 days after delivery. One request per order, reviewed by our team.
*   **Account Dashboard** — View orders, favorites, addresses, personal discounts, and support tickets in one place.

## 6. How We Grow — Marketing & Growth

### 6.1 Getting Found (Attracting New Customers)

*   **Google & SEO** — Fast pages, Persian content, and detailed product information help us appear high in search results. Our blog publishes style guides and fashion articles that bring new visitors who are searching for ideas.
*   **Social Proof** — Customer reviews on products build trust for the next visitor.
*   **Brand & Design** — A premium, consistent look (deep blue, warm cream, modern typography) and fully Persian, mobile-friendly experience make us memorable.
*   **Collections & Campaigns** — Seasonal collections and homepage banners highlight what's relevant now (e.g. Summer collection) and create urgency.

### 6.2 Turning Visitors into Buyers (Conversion)

*   **Personal Discounts** — Two ways to save:
    *   General or targeted promo codes (e.g. for first-time buyers, or customers in a specific city)
    *   **Negotiated discount in the cart** — Chat with the assistant to bargain for a personal, short-time offer. The offer only works if you keep those items in your cart, which encourages finishing the purchase.
*   **Affiliate Codes** — A customer's friend or favorite influencer gives them a code that gives both a discount and supports the partner.
*   **Help at the Right Moment** — The styling assistant appears when customers are unsure, answering questions instead of letting them leave.

### 6.3 Bringing Customers Back (Retention)

*   **Abandoned Cart Recovery** — If someone leaves items in the cart, we can send a gentle SMS reminder with a special offer to complete the purchase.
*   **Wishlist Notifications** — "The item you saved is now cheaper" or "Back in stock" — automated, personal reasons to return.
*   **Push & In-App Messages** — Updates about order status, payment, support replies, and new vouchers. Customers choose what they want to hear about.
*   **My Vouchers Page** — All personal discounts in one place, with expiry dates — a reason to come back before they expire.
*   **Blog & Inspiration** — Regular style content keeps the brand in the customer's mind even when they are not ready to buy.

### 6.4 Understanding Our Customers (Data)

*   **Behavior Insights** — We understand what customers view, save, add to cart, and buy — fully respecting privacy. This helps us see where people drop off and improve the experience.
*   **Sales Dashboard** — Daily view of revenue, orders, popular products, and voucher usage to make quick business decisions.
*   **Trending Products** — Automatically shows what is popular right now, so new visitors see what others love.

### 6.5 Everyday Marketing Tools (No Developer Needed)

The business team can, without technical help:

*   Change homepage banners, sliders, and featured collections
*   Create, edit, or stop any discount code or affiliate code
*   Publish or edit blog articles
*   Manage categories, brands, and product visibility
*   Reply to support tickets and approve reviews
*   Manage job openings and career applications

## 7. Why Customers Choose Voxcina (Competitive Edge)

1.  **See Before You Buy** — Virtual try-on is rare in Iran's fashion market. It solves the biggest fear: "Will it suit me?"
2.  **Talk, Don't Just Scroll** — Persian AI assistant that understands natural language and negotiates a personal price. Feels human, not like a filter menu.
3.  **Fully Local** — Language, dates, maps, payments, and shipping built for Iran — not a translated foreign template.
4.  **Trust & Service** — Clear photos per color, honest reviews, 7-day returns, and human support for questions.
5.  **Speed & Mobile** — Fast loading, especially on phones where most customers shop, with content cached across the country.

## 8. Operations Behind the Scenes

*   **One shop, full control** — We manage products, stock, orders, pricing, and content centrally. No third-party sellers to coordinate.
*   **Live stock** — Stock is checked at every step; customers never pay for something that is sold out.
*   **Support** — Customers can open support tickets; staff and managers answer from the same system.
*   **Careers & Growth** — We hire via the site (/careers) — applications with PDF resumes are securely stored and receive an automatic reply.
*   **Affiliate Management** — Clear overview of each partner's codes, sales, and commissions.
